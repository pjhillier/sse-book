**Author:** Peter J. Hillier, CD, CISSP

This open-access technical book explains how Systems Security Engineering integrates cybersecurity, resilience and assurance into established systems engineering over the system life cycle. It is intended for systems engineers, security professionals, acquisition practitioners, technical managers and educators.

**Publication files**

- `The_Security_Engineering_Convergence_1.0.pdf`: paginated reference edition (337 pages).
- `The_Security_Engineering_Convergence_1.0.epub`: reflowable e-reader edition; author reports testing in Calibre and Thorium Reader.
- `SSE_Book_Companion_1.0.zip`: teaching examples and practitioner materials.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: integrity digests of the three assets.

The technical examples, including Python simulations, are teaching abstractions and must not be interpreted as verification of an operational or physical system.

Corrections and future editions will be identified through subsequent release versions.
